    SPA · VER · 01
    spatial verification, induction, oddity and evidence under the mask · prototype v22
    The core size scale is S · M · L · XL. Latin grids use numbers, colours, or shading patterns as their symbols. Every claim is judged from memory.
    How to play
    Watch the scene — it vanishes behind noise — then judge the claim from memory; nothing is shown again. A mistake opens a correction card with the real scene and the fact. A rung is passed at 7 of the last 8 once every question family of that rung has been met; the move applies on the next trial (4 of 8 steps down). Pick any unlocked rung below.
    Keys: ← / Y = yes · → / N = no · 1–4 = options · Esc = menu.
    Track A · Load
    Three rich objects as the rule, never more than five: attributes, positions, comparisons, bindings, sets, containers and pointing — negated frames from R3.
    Track B · Arity
    Sequences, qualified references, Latin grids, transformations of two pairs and closures over twins.
    Track C · Number
    Brief flashes: subitising, subsets, comparisons and exact differences — total area is controlled.
    Track D · Induction
    A finite public grammar — alternations, quarter and half turns, texture and size cycles, side counts — then hypothesis spaces, active tests, transfer to new dimensions, two-input gates and program equivalence.
    Track E · Relational oddity
    Four cards, one exception, one public explanation: relations, transformations, programs, gates and conditional counterexamples. Surface salience never counts by itself.
    Track F · Evidence
    Necessity is not truth: classify a claim as necessary, possible only or impossible (E0); then judge one named relation of five — critical, redundant or irrelevant (E1).
